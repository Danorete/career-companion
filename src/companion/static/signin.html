Sign in with Google

This is an invite-only test. Signing in shares only your email address with Link, and Link keeps a scrambled version of it, not the address itself. Connecting your calendar or email is a separate, optional step inside Link.

Where your information goes

During this test, Link keeps your information on Link’s server so your follow-ups work between visits. It’s deleted when you click Delete everything, or automatically after 30 days without signing in.

The person running this test could technically see what’s stored. A future version will keep everything on your own device.
